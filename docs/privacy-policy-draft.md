## What we collect
- **Mobile number**: used to sign you in (one-time SMS code).
- **Profile**: first and last name, date of birth, pincode (plus the district and state looked up from
  it), and optionally email, playing position, city and a profile photo.
- **Activity**: matches you play or score (scores, rallies, times), teams, challenges, tournaments,
  photos you add to a match, and a match or court location when you choose to share it.
- **Feedback** you send from Profile > Send feedback (stars, text, app version).
- **Notification token** if you allow notifications.
- We **never** ask for ID card numbers (Aadhaar, PAN, etc.) or payment details. Points have no money value.

## How we use it
Signing you in; running matches, teams, rankings and tournaments; checking results for fair play
(e.g. the opposing captain confirms, unusual patterns are flagged for admin review); showing your age
group and area; sending notifications you asked for; improving the app from feedback.
